The platform

Built for how attendance
actually happens.

Not a punch clock bolted onto a spreadsheet. A system that verifies who, where, and when — then turns that straight into payroll.

Face ID, on-device

Employees verify with a glance — the camera confirms it's them and the photo never leaves the phone. Fingerprint works the same way where the hardware supports it.

GPS-verified check-ins

Every check-in and check-out carries a location stamp, visible to Admin and HR — so "I was at the site" is something you can actually confirm.

Multi-tenant, by design

One Super Admin account, unlimited companies underneath it. Each company's data, roles and reports stay completely walled off from the next.

Leave, end to end

Requests, approvals, rejections and running balances — synced the instant they happen between the employee's phone and the admin console.

Payroll that keeps up

Payslips generate straight from attendance data and download as PDF — one month at a time, or a whole year bundled into a single file.

An app the team keeps

A native mobile experience employees don't dread opening — fast check-ins, clear balances, and a home screen that actually tells them something.

Access, sorted by who needs what

Four layers.
Zero confusion.

Permissions follow the org chart you already have — and each company's Admin decides exactly what their own HR team can touch.

01

Super Admin

Creates and oversees every company on the platform — the only role that isn't tied to one workforce.

02

Admin

Runs their company end to end — departments, shifts, payroll, devices, and what HR is allowed to do.

03

HR

Handles the daily operations — employees, attendance, leave — within permissions their own Admin set.

04

Employee

Self-service only: their own check-ins, leave requests, and payslips, from the phone in their pocket.
